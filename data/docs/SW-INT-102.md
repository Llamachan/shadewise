# Hearthline Classic Matt Emulsion

SKU: SW-INT-102
Category: Interior wall paint, matt
Coverage: 110 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 10 L, 20 L

## Best for
Ceilings, rental repaints and low-traffic rooms. The flat finish hides minor wall imperfections. Not recommended for areas that need frequent scrubbing.

## Surface preparation
Walls must be clean, dry and primed. Fill cracks with wall putty and sand smooth. On walls with damp history, use Hearthline DampGuard Primer (SW-PRM-301) first.

## Application
Apply 2 coats by brush or roller. Thin the first coat with up to 15% water and the second with up to 10%.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 30 minutes. Wait 3 hours between coats.
