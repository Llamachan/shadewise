# Hearthline DampGuard Primer

SKU: SW-PRM-301
Category: Primer, interior and exterior masonry
Coverage: 90 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 10 L

## Best for
Walls with damp patches, mild seepage or efflorescence (white salt deposits). Apply before any Hearthline top coat on walls that have shown moisture.

## Surface preparation
Fix the source of water first (leaking pipes, cracked plaster, roof seepage). Scrape off loose paint and salt deposits with a wire brush. Let the wall dry for at least 7 days after repairs.

## Application
Apply 1 coat by brush or roller. Do not thin more than 5% with water.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 1 hour. Recoat or top coat after 6 hours.
