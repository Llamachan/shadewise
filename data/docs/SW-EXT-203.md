# Hearthline TerraceGuard Roof Coat

SKU: SW-EXT-203
Category: Exterior waterproof coating, terraces and roofs, heat-reflective white
Coverage: 35 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 4 L, 10 L, 20 L

## Best for
Flat concrete terraces, roof slabs and sunshades (chajjas). Seals the slab against rain and reflects sunlight, reducing heat in the room below. Suitable for light foot traffic only.

## Surface preparation
Sweep and wash off dust and moss. Clear drain outlets so water does not pool. Fill cracks and gaps around pipes with waterproof crack filler. Let the slab dry for 2 sunny days. No separate primer is needed.

## Application
Apply 3 coats by brush. Thin the first coat with 30% water so it soaks in and acts as the primer. Apply the second and third coats unthinned, each brushed at right angles to the one before. Do not apply if rain is expected within 24 hours.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 2 hours. Wait 6 hours between coats. Avoid foot traffic for 3 days; fully cured in 7 days.
