# Hearthline Deck & Door Exterior Wood Stain

SKU: SW-WOD-402
Category: Wood finish, exterior, translucent tinted stain
Coverage: 100 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 10 L

## Best for
Exterior doors, window frames, decks, pergolas and garden furniture. Soaks into the wood, repels water and blocks the UV that turns timber grey, while keeping the grain visible.

## Surface preparation
Wood must be bare or previously stained, not painted or varnished. Sand weathered grey wood back to fresh fibres with 120-grit, then 180-grit paper. Wait for 3 dry days after rain before staining. No primer is needed.

## Application
Stir well; do not thin. Apply 2 coats by brush along the grain, or 3 coats on decks and other horizontal surfaces. Wipe off any stain that has not soaked in after 15 minutes to avoid a sticky film. Reapply 1 coat every 2 to 3 years.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 4 hours. Wait 12 hours between coats. Rain resistant after 24 hours; decks can be walked on after 48 hours.
