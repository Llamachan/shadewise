# Hearthline Timbercoat PU Clear Finish

SKU: SW-WOD-401
Category: Wood finish, clear polyurethane, gloss, interior
Coverage: 120 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 20 L

## Best for
Interior furniture, doors, cabinets and panelling. The clear, hard-wearing film shows the natural grain and resists scratches, water rings and household stains.

## Surface preparation
Remove old polish or varnish completely. Sand bare wood along the grain with 180-grit, then 240-grit paper. Fill gaps with matching wood filler and wipe off all dust. Do not use a primer. For a darker tone, apply a wood stain first and let it dry for 12 hours.

## Application
Apply 3 coats by brush. Thin the first coat with up to 20% PU thinner to act as a sealer; thin later coats up to 10%. Sand lightly with 320-grit paper between coats.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 2 hours. Wait 6 hours between coats. Ready for light use after 24 hours, full hardness in 7 days.
