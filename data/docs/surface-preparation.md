# Hearthline Surface Preparation Guide

Category: Guide, all surfaces
Applies to: Every Hearthline paint, primer, wood finish and enamel

## Before you start
Fix the source of any water first: leaking pipes, cracked plaster, roof seepage. To check a surface is dry, tape a 30 cm square of plastic sheet to it for 24 hours. Moisture under the plastic means it needs more drying time.

## Interior walls
Scrape off loose or flaking paint. Fill cracks and holes with wall putty, let it harden, then sand smooth. Wash off grease and mould, and wipe away dust before priming.

## Exterior walls
Scrub off dirt, algae and chalky residue with water and a stiff brush, then let the wall dry for 2 sunny days. Fill cracks with exterior crack filler. New plaster must cure for 28 days before painting.

## Wood
Sand along the grain until smooth. Fill knots and gaps with wood putty. Remove old varnish completely before applying a clear finish. Wait for dry weather after rain.

## Metal
Remove rust and loose paint with a wire brush or emery paper. Degrease with mineral turpentine and prime the same day, before fresh rust forms.

## Choosing a primer
Masonry walls, inside or out: DampGuard Primer (SW-PRM-301). Bare wood and metal under enamel: Multi-Surface Primer (SW-PRM-302). Clear finishes (SW-WOD-401) and wood stains (SW-WOD-402): no primer. Terraces: the thinned first coat of TerraceGuard Roof Coat (SW-EXT-203) acts as the primer.
