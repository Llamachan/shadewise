# Hearthline WeatherShield Max

SKU: SW-EXT-202
Category: Exterior wall paint, elastomeric, crack-bridging
Coverage: 50 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 10 L, 20 L

## Best for
Exterior walls with hairline cracks and heavy rain exposure, such as coastal homes, hill regions and west-facing walls. The thick, flexible film stretches to bridge hairline cracks up to 0.5 mm and keeps driving rain out.

## Surface preparation
Wash off dirt and algae, then let the wall dry for 2 sunny days. Fill cracks wider than 0.5 mm with exterior crack filler. Prime with Hearthline DampGuard Primer (SW-PRM-301).

## Application
Apply 2 coats. Do not thin more than 5% with water. Lay the paint on thick and even; spreading it too far reduces the waterproof film. Do not paint if rain is expected within 6 hours.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 2 hours. Wait 6 hours between coats. Rain resistant after 24 hours, fully cured in 7 days.
