# Hearthline WeatherShield Exterior Emulsion

SKU: SW-EXT-201
Category: Exterior wall paint, weatherproof, low sheen
Coverage: 80 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 10 L, 20 L

## Best for
Exterior plastered walls, compound walls and balconies. The acrylic film resists monsoon rain, UV fading and algae growth.

## Surface preparation
Wash off dirt, algae and chalky residue, then let the wall dry for 2 sunny days. Fill cracks with exterior crack filler. New plaster must cure for 28 days. Prime new or powdery plaster with Hearthline DampGuard Primer (SW-PRM-301).

## Application
Apply 2 coats. Thin the first coat with up to 15% water and the second with up to 10%. Do not paint in direct midday sun or if rain is expected within 6 hours.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 1 hour. Wait 4 hours between coats. Rain resistant after 24 hours.
