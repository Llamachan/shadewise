# Hearthline Kitchen & Bath Emulsion

SKU: SW-INT-103
Category: Interior wall paint, satin, anti-fungal
Coverage: 110 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 10 L

## Best for
Kitchens, bathrooms and utility areas. The anti-fungal film resists mould in steamy rooms and wipes clean of grease splashes. Not for surfaces in direct contact with running water, such as shower walls.

## Surface preparation
Wash off existing mould with 1 part household bleach to 4 parts water, rinse, and let dry for 24 hours. Degrease walls near the stove with mild detergent. Prime with Hearthline DampGuard Primer (SW-PRM-301).

## Application
Apply 2 coats by brush or roller. Thin with up to 10% water. Keep the room ventilated while painting and drying.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 1 hour. Wait 4 hours between coats. Avoid steam from showers or cooking for 48 hours.
