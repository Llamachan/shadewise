# Hearthline Silk Emulsion

SKU: SW-INT-101
Category: Interior wall paint, soft sheen
Coverage: 120 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 10 L, 20 L

## Best for
Living rooms and bedrooms. Washable finish that resists light stains.

## Surface preparation
Walls must be clean, dry and primed. On walls with damp history, use Hearthline DampGuard Primer (SW-PRM-301) first.

## Application
Apply 2 coats. Thin the first coat with up to 10% water.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 30 minutes. Wait 4 hours between coats.
