# Hearthline Gloss Enamel

SKU: SW-ENM-501
Category: Enamel, high gloss, solvent-based, interior and exterior
Coverage: 150 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 20 L

## Best for
Metal grills, gates and railings, and wooden doors and windows, indoors or outdoors. Dries to a hard, glossy, rust-resistant film.

## Surface preparation
Prime bare wood and metal with Hearthline Multi-Surface Primer (SW-PRM-302). On old enamel, sand to dull the gloss and wipe clean. Remove any fresh rust before painting.

## Application
Apply 2 coats by brush or spray. Thin up to 10% with mineral turpentine for brushing, or up to 20% for spraying. Brush out well on vertical surfaces to avoid runs. Keep the area ventilated.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 4 hours. Recoat after 16 hours.
