# Hearthline AquaSatin Enamel

SKU: SW-ENM-502
Category: Enamel, satin, water-based, low odour
Coverage: 130 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 20 L

## Best for
Interior doors, window frames, skirting, furniture and kitchen cabinets where low smell and quick recoating matter. Whites stay white without yellowing. Not recommended for exterior metal.

## Surface preparation
Prime bare wood and metal with Hearthline Multi-Surface Primer (SW-PRM-302). Over old solvent-based enamel, sand to a dull finish and wipe clean, or the new coat may not grip.

## Application
Apply 2 coats by brush or roller. Thin with up to 5% water. Work in small sections and do not brush back into paint that has started to set. Clean tools with water.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 1 hour. Wait 4 hours between coats. Handle gently for 3 days while the film hardens.
