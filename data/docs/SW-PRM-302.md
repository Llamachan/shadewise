# Hearthline Multi-Surface Primer

SKU: SW-PRM-302
Category: Primer, wood and metal, solvent-based
Coverage: 110 sq ft per litre per coat
Pack sizes: 1 L, 4 L, 10 L

## Best for
Bare wood (doors, windows, furniture) and iron or steel (grills, gates, railings) before Hearthline Gloss Enamel (SW-ENM-501) or AquaSatin Enamel (SW-ENM-502). Contains rust inhibitors. Not for masonry walls; use Hearthline DampGuard Primer (SW-PRM-301) there. Not for use under clear finishes or wood stains.

## Surface preparation
Wood: sand smooth with 180-grit paper, fill knots and cracks with wood putty, and wipe off dust. Metal: remove rust and loose paint with a wire brush or emery paper, then degrease with mineral turpentine.

## Application
Apply 1 coat on wood. On bare metal in coastal or humid areas, apply 2 coats. Thin up to 10% with mineral turpentine.

## Drying time
Touch dry in 1 hour. Recoat or top coat after 8 hours. Lightly sand primed wood before the top coat.
